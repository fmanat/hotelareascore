**Interpretation:** the dedupe pass (`docs/adr/002` rule) deliberately refuses to merge records whose names don't normalize to the same string, even at the same coordinates — merging distinct branches or sub-venues of a chain is explicitly forbidden. Coincident coordinates with distinct names are therefore surfaced, not silently fixed. Two patterns observed in this run: (a) a single hotel's sub-venues (restaurant, named suites, the legal entity name) each carrying their own `hotel`-family taxonomy leaf in Overture — cosmetic, low product risk; (b) in Bangkok, a small number of clusters that look like travel-directory/aggregator listings sharing one fallback coordinate rather than real distinct hotels — worth a manual spot-check before these are ever made indexable.
