## 1. What this site is — and is not

This site provides **automated, data-derived information about the
surroundings of hotels**. Scores are calculated automatically from geospatial
data using the methodology described on our [Methodology](/methodology) page.
**They describe the surrounding area, not hotel rooms, service, or quality.**

The site is **not**: a booking service, a travel agency, a review platform, a
safety rating, or a measurement of actual noise levels. The "quiet
surroundings" dimension is an **estimate of environmental noise exposure from
nearby infrastructure and activity — not an in-room noise measurement.**

## 2. Accuracy — honest limits

Scores are computed from third-party open data that can be incomplete,
outdated, or wrong (hotels close, move, or are misrecorded at the source).
Every score page shows its data date, score version, and a confidence level —
read them. Scores are **decision support, not guarantees**. Always verify
essentials (location, existence, suitability) with the hotel or booking
platform before booking. To the fullest extent permitted by law, FrenchSquare
Ltd accepts no liability for booking decisions made on the basis of the
information on this site.

## 3. Corrections

If you believe a hotel's data or score is wrong (wrong location, closed
hotel, duplicate, implausible score), write to **[contact email]** with the
page link. We review every report; corrections that pass verification are
applied at the next data release, and material changes update the page's
"last updated" date. We do not, however, change scores on request without a
data basis — including requests from hotels themselves.

## 4. Acceptable use

You may use the site for personal, non-automated purposes. You may not

evasion, automated harvesting, and misrepresenting our scores as your own are
prohibited. (An API or embeddable widget may be offered separately in the
future — those uses will have their own terms.) Short quotations of
individual scores with attribution and a link are welcome.
