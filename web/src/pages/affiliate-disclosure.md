Some outbound links on this site — typically the "See rates at…" buttons on
hotel pages — are **affiliate links**. If you click one and later make a
booking on the partner's site, FrenchSquare Ltd may receive a commission from
that partner, at **no extra cost to you**. These links are marked with
`rel="sponsored"` in the page code, as search-engine guidelines require.

## What affiliate relationships never touch

Our location scores are calculated automatically from geospatial data using a
published methodology. **Affiliate relationships never influence a score, a
ranking, a verdict, or which hotels appear on this site.** Concretely:

- Scores are computed before, and completely independently of, whether a
  hotel can be linked to a booking partner at all.
- A hotel with no affiliate partner gets exactly the same score treatment as
  one with several.
- No hotel, chain, or booking platform can pay to alter or remove a score.
  Scores are not for sale.
- Sponsored placements, if ever introduced, will be visually separate from
  scores and clearly labelled.
