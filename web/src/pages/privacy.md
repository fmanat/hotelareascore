## What we collect

**Nothing that identifies you personally.** In detail:

- **Aggregate, cookieless analytics** (Cloudflare Web Analytics): page views,
  referrers, country-level location, browser type. No cookies, no persistent
  identifiers, no cross-site tracking.
- **First-party usage events**: which hotels are searched and which outbound
  links are clicked, recorded without any account, name, or profile. We use
  these only to understand which pages are useful and to measure the site's
  own funnel.
- **Server logs** kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability,
  under the provider's own retention rules.

We do **not** use advertising trackers, social-media pixels, fingerprinting,
or any third-party analytics requiring consent.

## Cookies

The site sets **no cookies** requiring consent. If a strictly necessary
technical cookie is ever introduced (e.g. a preference toggle), it will be
listed here.

## Outbound booking links

Some pages contain outbound links to hotel booking partners (marked
`sponsored`). When you click one, you leave this site and the partner's own
privacy policy applies. The link may contain a non-personal tag telling the
partner the click came from us — this is how the site is funded (see our
[Affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure)). We do not transmit any
personal data with these clicks.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR/UK GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, and objection. Because we hold no account data and
no directly identifying data about visitors, in most cases there is simply
nothing to retrieve or delete — but you can always write to
**[contact email]** and we will answer within one month. You may also lodge a
complaint with your local supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL; in the
UK, the ICO).

## If this ever changes

Before enabling any feature that requires consent (accounts, embedded maps
with third-party cookies, consent-based analytics, advertising), this policy
will be updated and a compliant consent mechanism added **first**. The
current version of this page always reflects what the site actually does.
